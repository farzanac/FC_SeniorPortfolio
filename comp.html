GROUP COMPEITION PROJECTS

These are my favorite projects that I made from Unity

Talking Clean

Talking Clean was a group project for the Infy App Competition. My group created this app as a way to teach individuals about how to keep the earth clean and why. Specifly the app teaches the user about the three R's (Reuse, Reduce, Recycle) and how to use this knowledge in public areas. With lots of time and effort put into our research we were able to constuct an app surrounding the concept that is often ignored, Land Pollution, Manicipal Waste.

LemonAid

For this project, I worked in a group where we came together and created the app LemonAid. It is an educational app for kindergarten through fifth-grade students that uses interactive questions and a customizable reward system to motivate children to practice and reinforce what they learn in school.It is suppose to connect students with their parents so their parents are able to give extra help and see their child progress in their education at home. The goal of our app is to make learning outside the class more engaging and help children build stronger understanding through regular practice.
